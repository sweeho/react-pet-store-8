## Why

Recover the order-approval behaviour of the legacy Pet Store: automatic approval by locale and total, manual approve/deny by an administrator, the approval document exchange, and related notification and reporting rules.

## What Changes

- Adds the order-approval capability spec (rules, admin workflow, service, document, view, one screen).

## Impact

Affects order processing, the admin client and its XML-over-HTTP service, and order notification email.
