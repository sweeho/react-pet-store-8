## Why

Recover the checkout and payment behaviour of the legacy pet store (order submission, contact validation, credit card model, order records and status) as a reviewable specification.

## What Changes

- Adds the `checkout-payment` capability spec with 29 requirements, one per extracted record, none yet approved.
- Several records are twin readings of the same behaviour from two passes; an SME merges them on review.

## Impact

Specification only; no code is changed.
