## Why

Recover the supplier-fulfilment behaviour of the legacy Pet Store: dispatching supplier purchase orders on approval, fulfilling them against supplier inventory, invoicing, the administrator stock-update screen, and the stored data and document formats behind them.

## What Changes

- Adds the `supplier-fulfilment` capability spec (69 requirements, 1 screen requirement).
- Records stated legacy behaviour only; defects and conflicts are flagged, not corrected.

## Impact

Affects order processing (approval hand-off, invoice receipt), supplier inventory and the order/purchase-order data model.
