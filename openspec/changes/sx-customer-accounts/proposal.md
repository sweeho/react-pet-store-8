# Proposal: customer accounts

## Why

Recover the behaviour of the legacy customer, account, profile, contact and address handling so it can be rebuilt and verified.

## What changes

- Specifies customer creation and update, account status, profile preferences and defaults, contact info and address rules, duplicate user handling, locales and the my-list panel.
- Records schema constraints as requirements.

## Impact

New capability `customer-accounts`. Three records are disputed and need Business Owner review.
